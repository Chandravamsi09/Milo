⚔ HOW TO PLAY

🎮 Controls

  • WASD / ↑↓←→ Move Milo across the dungeon
  • Shift Invulnerable Dash (0.4s i-frames)
  • Left Click Cast Magic Bolt at target

⚡ Spells & Abilities

  • 1 Magic Bolt (10 MP) — Fast ranged bolt
  • 2 Fire Burst (25 MP) — Area of effect explosion
  • 3 Lightning (40 MP) — Targeted high single-target strike
  • 4 Ultimate (60 MP) — Screen-clearing shockwave

💎 Mission Objective

1. Collect 3 Glowing Blue Elemental Crystals (+35 MP, +20 HP restore each).

2. Survive 3 waves of Goblins, Sorcerers, and Fire Drakes.

3. Defeat the 3-Phase Dungeon Guardian Boss!
